SITUATION
After cleaning the order data, total revenue stands at Rs 97,358.30. The raw figure was Rs 99,860.20; the Rs 2,501.90 difference comes entirely from duplicate orders caused by a double-submit bug, not from any real loss of sales. March 2026 is the genuine peak month at Rs 20,318.90.

COMPLICATION
Returns are not spread evenly. Cash on delivery orders are returned at 44.4%, against 18.9% for UPI and 14.7% for card. The problem is concentrated further: COD orders in Tier-2 cities are returned at 54.5%, the highest of any segment. The revenue trend also misleads at first glance: January 2026 appears to lead at Rs 29,582.10, but that is inflated by two bulk orders and falls to Rs 11,637.10 once they are excluded.

RESOLUTION
Ops should target COD orders in Tier-2 cities first, for example with order confirmation calls, or by nudging those customers toward prepaid payment. Finance should report revenue on the de-duplicated base of Rs 97,358.30, flag bulk orders separately so they do not distort monthly trends, and plan inventory and campaigns around March 2026 as the real peak.
